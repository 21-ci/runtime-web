# Members

Members are people with access to an org. You give them an org role, and then project roles for the projects they
should work on.

## Invite a person

1. Open **Settings > Members** and invite the person by email, with an org role.
2. Send them the invite link the console shows. The link is single use and expires after 7 days.

If the person has no account yet, the invite leads them through sign-up. If they already have one, they sign in and
accept the invite. Pending invites are listed until they are accepted, revoked or expire.

## Change a role

Owners and admins can change a member's org role. Only an owner can grant or remove the owner role. The console refuses
a change that would leave the org without an owner.

## Give project access

Open a member and set a project role for each project they should reach: `admin`, `developer` or `viewer`. Remove a
grant to take that access away. Org owners and admins act as project admins in every project.

## Remove a member

Removing a member also removes their project grants.
